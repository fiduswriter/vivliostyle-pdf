JetBrains Mono (SIL Open Font License 1.1)
===========================================

JetBrainsMono-Regular.ttf and JetBrainsMono-Bold.ttf are bundled
(in public/fonts/) as the monospace fallback family: the last-resort font for
monospace text runs whose `@font-face` fonts cannot be embedded, and — via the
emitter's per-glyph fallback — a source of glyphs missing from a run's primary
font (math symbols, arrows, etc.).

They are used by:
- `src/pdf-emitter.ts` (`FALLBACK_FONT_FILES.monospace`); and
- the demo document / end-to-end tests that rely on monospace fallback.

alongside these files as `OFL.txt` for the OFL-licensed fonts).
