Noto Sans Arabic and Noto Sans Hebrew (SIL Open Font License 1.1)
===============================================================

These fonts are bundled (in public/fonts/) to give right-to-left (Arabic /
Hebrew) glyph coverage to the demo document and the end-to-end tests, and for
any Hebrew/Arabic document that references them via `@font-face`.

They are used by:
- the demo document (`src/demo-document.html`, the "Typography, Direction &
  Decoration" section, families "Noto Sans Arabic" / "Noto Sans Hebrew"); and
- `test/rtl.spec.ts` (RTL output verification).

at https://openfontlicense.org (a copy also lives alongside these files as
`OFL.txt` for the OFL-licensed fonts).
