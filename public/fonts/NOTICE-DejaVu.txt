DejaVu Sans (SIL Open Font License 1.1)
========================================

DejaVu Sans-Regular.ttf is bundled (in public/fonts/) so the demo document and
tests can showcase dynamic `@font-face` font discovery — a distinct, non-serif
family that is picked up automatically by the emitter rather than mapped from
a hardcoded registry.
