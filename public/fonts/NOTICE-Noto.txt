Noto Sans Arabic and Noto Sans Hebrew (SIL Open Font License 1.1)
================================================================

These fonts are bundled (in public/fonts/) to give the end-to-end **tests**
(`test/rtl.spec.ts`) and any Hebrew/Arabic document a font with RTL glyph
coverage. They are not used by the default demo document.
